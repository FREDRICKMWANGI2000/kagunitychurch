Welcome home

A peaceful place to grow in faith

K.A.G Unity Church is a welcoming place of worship near the Thika Superhighway in Ruiru.

Known for its peaceful environment, clean surroundings and beautiful floral interior, it is a place where you can experience God’s presence and grow spiritually. We are part of the Kenya Assemblies of God, a movement rooted in faith, prayer and community service.

Whether you are seeking salvation, spiritual growth or a warm community of believers, you are welcome here. Our doors are open to all, so come as you are.

Our Covenant & Identity · Kenya Assemblies of God

Rooted in the Word,
alive in the Spirit

Inscribed on our church emblem are three words that anchor every service, prayer and outreach at Toll Ruiru: “All the Gospel.”

“For where two or three gather in my name, there am I with them.”

Matthew 18:20

Step through the blue gates just off the Thika Superhighway and you enter a sanctuary set apart for prayer, renewal and genuine Christian fellowship. K.A.G Unity Church – Toll Ruiru is a local assembly of the Kenya Assemblies of God, standing as a spiritual home for individuals, youth and families across Ruiru.

We believe the church is not merely a building, but a living family of believers redeemed by grace, grounded in Scripture, and empowered by the Holy Spirit to serve our neighbours with love and compassion.

Our Mission

Proclaiming Christ & Nourishing Souls

To preach the whole counsel of God’s Word, lead people into a saving relationship with Jesus Christ, and disciple believers through vibrant worship, prayer and fellowship.

Our Vision

A United, Spirit-Filled Community

To be a house of prayer for all people in Ruiru and beyond—raising Christ-centred families, purposeful youth and servant leaders who transform their community.

The Four Cornerstones of Our Fellowship

  1. I Hebrews 11:1

    Faith

    Rooted in the Word of God and anchored by unwavering belief in the saving power of Jesus Christ.

  2. II Acts 2:42

    Community

    A welcoming family bound together by love, mutual care and authentic Christian fellowship.

  3. III John 4:24

    Worship

    Passionate, Spirit-filled praise and worship that ushers every heart into God’s living presence.

  4. IV 2 Timothy 3:16

    The Word

    Bible-centred preaching and teaching that transforms hearts, renews the mind and builds unshakable hope.
